## Full description (4,000 max)

```
Track your collection. Get free restock alerts. Built for collectors, not scalpers.

Code 3 TCG is one app for both halves of the hobby: keeping track of what you own, and finding what you're still looking for.

YOUR COLLECTION
• Add cards and sealed products by search, barcode number or camera
• See sets you're working on and what's still missing
• Keep a Wants list for the cards you're chasing
• Works for any trading card game, with more than one game side by side

FREE RESTOCK ALERTS
• Follow the stores you actually visit
• Get an alert when another collector reports a restock there
• Save a search and get an alert when a matching product is reported or its price drops
• A restock map shows stores near you and the latest report for each one
• Share what you saw in two taps: stocked, low, or an empty shelf

BUILT FOR COLLECTORS, NOT SCALPERS
• No checkout bots and no auto-buying
• No store delivery schedules or restock pattern history
• Reports are reviewed, and each one shows when it was seen and whether it was confirmed
• Teen accounts get a safer view: they can browse restocks and keep a collection, but cannot post reports or listings

FAIR PRICES
• Look up a card or product before you buy or trade
• Check whether a trade is fair

Reports come from other collectors and describe what they saw at that moment. Stock can change before you arrive, and Code 3 TCG is not affiliated with any retailer or game publisher.

Free to use. Create an account to save your collection and get alerts.
```

## Wording to change elsewhere in the listing

- Use "Wants", not "Wishlist", in every screenshot caption and description.
- Do not use "Deals" or "Auctions" in the listing: those are seller tools and
  are hidden from collectors in the app.

## Not claimed on purpose

- Scan accuracy or speed (not yet tested against a set of real cards).
- Live inventory or guaranteed stock (reports are observations).
- Number of users, stores or reports.
