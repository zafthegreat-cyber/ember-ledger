# Code 3 TCG Community Guidelines (draft for Zena's review, 2026-11-14)

Short enough to read on a phone, specific enough to enforce. The in-app Community,
the Discord server and restock reports all follow this one set. Items marked
**enforced** are already checked by code (`src/utils/communitySafety.js`,
`validateTidepoolPostDraft`, `validateMarketplaceListingDraft`); the rest are
reviewed by moderators.

## The one-line version

Be the person you'd want your kid to trade with.

## 1. Family first

- Kids use Code 3. Write every post as if an eight-year-old is reading it. No profanity, no adult jokes, no violence or threats.
- Never ask a child for a private message, photo, address or school. **Enforced:** "no parents", "DM the kid privately" and similar phrasings are blocked.
- Adults never arrange in-person meetups with minors through Code 3. Trades with kids go through a parent.

## 2. Honest cards, honest money

- No counterfeit, proxy, replica, resealed or weighed product — not selling, not trading, not "just asking". **Enforced.**
- No pushing people off platform or to unprotected payments: "Friends & Family only", "Zelle only", "no goods and services", gift cards, wire transfers. **Enforced.** Buyer protection exists for a reason; we don't help people get around it.
- Condition is described honestly, with photos of the actual item. "NM" means near mint, not "I think it looks fine".
- Prices are yours to set, but no fake "comps", no bidding up your own listing, no shill offers.

## 3. Restock reports people can trust

- Report what you actually saw, when you saw it. Store, time, shelf status and what was there are required.
- No guessing dressed up as fact. "Vendor seen" is not "restocked". The app has separate report types for a reason.
- No employee names, no vendor schedules, no store-internal information. Protecting the people who work there protects the reports.
- Don't post to drive people to a store so you can buy before them, and don't post false "empty" reports to keep people away.
- No scalper talk: "cleared the shelf", "got all 12", bulk-buy bragging, bot or resale-margin chatter. Code 3 exists so collectors can find product, not so a few can clear it.

## 4. No impersonation

- Don't pretend to be staff, a moderator, a shop or a brand. **Enforced:** usernames and posts that claim to be official Code 3 / Ember & Tide admin or support are blocked.
- Shops get a verified-shop label from the Code 3 team only; nobody can buy or claim one.

## 5. Respect

- Disagree about prices, grades or pulls all you like; don't attack the person. No harassment, no slurs, no dogpiling, no "exposing" people.
- Don't post other people's personal information, screenshots of private messages, or photos of people without consent.
- One account per person. No alt accounts to dodge a timeout.

## 6. What moderators do

- New posts may wait for review before they appear. Reports from members go to a queue; nothing is deleted silently without a reason the poster can read.
- First problem: the post is hidden and you get a note saying why. Second: a timeout. Pattern or anything in sections 1–2: removal, and for safety issues involving minors, a report to the appropriate authority.
- Appeals: reply to the moderation note or use Settings → Feedback. A different moderator reviews it.

## 7. Reporting

Every post, comment, listing and restock report has a Report action. Pick the closest reason; add a sentence if it helps. You'll hear back when it's reviewed.
