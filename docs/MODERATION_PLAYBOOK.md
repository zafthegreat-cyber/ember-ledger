# Moderation playbook (draft for Zena's review, 2026-11-14)

For whoever is holding the moderator role that day: Zena, Dillon, or a trusted
volunteer. It covers the in-app Community, restock reports, marketplace listings
and the Discord server. Pair with `COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md` (what members see)
and `PLAY_DATA_SAFETY_ANSWERS.md` (what we told Google).

## Daily (10 minutes)

1. Open **Admin → Review** in the app. Work the queues top to bottom: *New posts awaiting review* → *Member reports* → *Restock reports flagged* → *Listings flagged*.
2. In Discord, check `#mod-log` and the report reactions.
3. Anything involving a minor's safety: stop, follow section 4 first.
4. Log what you did (the queue records actions; for Discord, one line in `#mod-log`).

Target: nothing sits in a queue longer than 24 hours during beta.

## Decision ladder

| Severity | Examples | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fix** | Wrong store on a report, typo'd price, missing photo | Approve with a note, or ask the poster to edit | Any mod |
| **Hide** | Off-topic, scalper brag, mild rudeness, unverifiable "restocked" | Hide + canned note (below). First offence only. | Any mod |
| **Timeout** | Second hide in 30 days, arguing in replies after a hide, borderline sales push | Hide + 7-day posting timeout + note | Any mod, log it |
| **Remove** | Counterfeits/resealed, off-platform payment pressure, impersonating staff, harassment, doxxing, alt accounts | Remove content, disable posting, note; Zena reviews the account within 48 h | Mod flags, Zena confirms |
| **Safety** | Anything sexual, grooming-shaped or coercive toward a minor; threats of violence; self-harm | Section 4 immediately | Zena or Dillon |

When in doubt, hide and ask another mod. Hiding is reversible; a member seeing something harmful is not.

## Canned notes (copy, then personalise one line)

- **Hidden — off platform:** "We hid your post because it asked for Friends & Family / app-only payment. Buyer protection matters here, especially for kids and parents. Repost without the payment line and it's fine."
- **Hidden — counterfeit wording:** "We don't allow replica, proxy, resealed or weighed product in any form, including questions about them. This post is hidden. If you were warning others, say so in the report tool instead."
- **Hidden — unverifiable restock:** "Your report said 'restocked' but didn't include what was on the shelf or when. We hid it so it doesn't send people to an empty store. You can resubmit with the shelf status and time."
- **Hidden — tone:** "This crossed from disagreeing to attacking. Hidden. Say the same thing about the cards, not the person, and it's welcome."
- **Timeout:** "This is the second hidden post in a month, so posting is paused for 7 days. Reading, trading history and your Vault are unaffected. Reply here if you think we got it wrong; a different moderator will look."
- **Removed:** "Your posting access has been removed for [reason]. Your account and collection are untouched. If you want to appeal, reply here; Zena reviews every removal within two days."

## Appeals

- A member replies to the note or uses Settings → Feedback (it lands in `beta_feedback` and Discord).
- A **different** moderator than the one who acted reviews it. Zena is the tie-break.
- Outcomes: restore (with an apology if we were wrong), uphold (one sentence why), or reduce (timeout shortened).
- Appeals are answered within 72 hours. Decisions and reasons are logged.

## 4. Child safety protocol

If content or behaviour suggests an adult is trying to contact, groom, pressure, or obtain images or information from a minor:

1. **Preserve first.** Do not delete. Screenshot the content, the account, timestamps and any linked accounts. Save to the private moderation folder (not Discord).
2. **Protect.** Remove the adult account's posting and messaging access. Hide the content from public view (hide, not delete).
3. **Notify.** In the US, report to NCMEC's CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org). If a child appears to be in immediate danger, call local law enforcement. Tell the child's parent if the family is known to us through a Family plan.
4. **Record.** Who did what and when, in the private log. Only Zena and Dillon handle these cases.
5. **Do not** investigate on your own, contact the adult, or discuss it in public channels.

For threats of violence: preserve, remove access, and call law enforcement if the threat is specific. For self-harm content: hide, reply with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US) in a private note, and do not lecture.

## Restock-report specifics

- Approve when: store is real, time is plausible, status matches the detail or photo.
- Needs edit when: product category missing, time in the future, store duplicate.
- Hide when: "restocked" with nothing on the shelf, obvious scalper brag, employee named, vendor schedule, or the same person posting the same store "empty" repeatedly.
- Never publish raw pattern history, vendor days or quantities beyond what the shop approved (AGENTS.md).

## Discord specifics

- Mods have 2FA on (server requirement).
- Use timeouts over kicks. Ban only for section-4 cases or repeat removals.
- Interest roles are opt-in; never mass-ping.
- Keep `#mod-log` factual: who, what, which rule, link.

## Conflicts of interest

If the post is about your own listing, trade or store, hand it to another mod. If a report is about a mod, Zena handles it.
