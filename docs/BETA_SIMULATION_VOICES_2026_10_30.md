# Simulated beta voices: 1,000 tester statements (October 30, 2026)

Synthetic, not real feedback. These are written in the voice of the simulated
beta population (family parents, kids with a parent, casual collectors, active
and power sellers, scouts, newcomers) and grounded in what the two 100,000-user
simulations and the Feature Status Matrix showed. Use them as a review checklist
and copy-testing bank, not as evidence of what real testers said.

250 requests.
